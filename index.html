How it works

Set up once on the iPad, then the show runs offline.

1 · Import the media pack

Put the reef-karaoke-media folder on the iPad (AirDrop it, or copy it to iCloud Drive). Tap Import media pack and pick the single reef-karaoke-media.zip file (or open the folder and select all the files). Every backing track, music video and background loads at once and stays saved in this browser.

Moving to another device

Tap My data, then Download data file. On the other device, import that file (the Import media pack button takes it too). It only adds: anything newer wins, nothing gets deleted. Media files move separately with the media pack zip.

2 · Synced timing is built in

Songs marked Synced already know when every line starts, read from the karaoke videos. Songs marked Estimate guess; tap Setup, then Record timing, and tap through one run to lock them in.

2 · Play YouTube alongside

This page can't see what the YouTube app is doing, since iPad apps can't read each other's playback. Press Play here and in YouTube together, then tap the lyrics as the first line starts. That tap locks the timing to the track; tap again any time it slips.

3 · Fix a song that drifts

If the words fall behind or run ahead partway through, pause on the first line that's off, tap Setup, then Fix from the current line. The track backs up a few seconds; tap the big button as each line starts, and Save once it's back in step. Lines after your last tap keep their timing, shifted to follow you. Record timing redoes a whole song from the top.

Music videos and backgrounds

For songs sung as written, load the instrumental as the backing track and the official video under Music video. The video plays silently, locked to the track, with the lyrics on top. Background video on the set list swaps the reef for any looping clip.

During the show

Tap the lyrics to jump to the next line, Previous to go back, and ±¼s to shift everything slightly. Space plays or pauses on a keyboard; arrow keys move lines.

Background video

Swap the animated reef behind every song for a looping video. It plays silently and stays saved in this browser.

Songs with their own music video show that video instead.
